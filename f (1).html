Regulatory Compliance

BUILT FOR SA REGULATION

Compliance is not an add-on — it is the foundation. Every module, certificate, and report in Digital Trainer is designed to satisfy South African regulatory requirements out of the box.

POPIA
Protection of Personal Information Act
All learning data is processed in accordance with POPIA. Staff data-handling training generates certificates admissible as POPIA compliance evidence before the Information Regulator.
✓ Fully Compliant
B-BBEE
Broad-Based Black Economic Empowerment
Automated tracking of skills development spend for Element 6 of the B-BBEE scorecard. Generate audit-ready reports for your verification agency with zero manual effort.
✓ Scorecard Ready
SETA
Sector Education & Training Authorities
Programmes aligned with SETA unit standards and qualifications. Enables employers to claim skills levy reimbursements and meet mandatory grant requirements through accredited training delivery.
✓ SETA Aligned
FSCA
Financial Sector Conduct Authority
Cybersecurity and conduct training modules map directly to FSCA Guidance Note 3 of 2022. Incident response simulations produce audit-ready logs demonstrating staff competency to supervisors.
✓ FSCA Aligned
